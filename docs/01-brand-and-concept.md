# 01 — Brand and concept

## Positioning

**What we are:** an engineering company whose foundation is data. We build the pipelines and platforms first, then the intelligence on top, then the products people actually use, and we stay to run them.

**What we are not:** an AI wrapper, a body shop, or a slide-deck consultancy.

**The proof structure** every page should follow: problem in the client's words → what we build → how it's architected → what changes for the business.

**Three pillars**

| Pillar | Role in the story | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| Data engineering | The foundation. Our strongest claim. | Pink |
| AI and intelligent systems | Built on the foundation, never floating above it. | Violet |
| Business development and digital products | Where it turns into something the business uses. | Ink |

## The concept: the mark is the architecture

The logo is a neuron: data arriving along many dendrites, converging at a soma where it changes colour, and leaving as a single black chain. That is literally what the company does:

- **Pink spokes** — fragmented sources arriving (ERP, CRM, sensors, documents, APIs).
- **The soma** — the platform where data is unified and transformed. Its pink-to-violet gradient is the moment data becomes intelligence.
- **Violet spokes** — models, knowledge, AI.
- **The black chain** — the engineered path out: decisions, actions, outcomes.

So the website doesn't decorate itself with generic "AI" imagery. It uses its own mark as the diagram language. Every architecture visual on the site is drawn in the same vocabulary as the logo: round nodes, straight edges, a ring that contains the system.

## The one bold thing

**The network hero.** The homepage opens with the mark rebuilt at full scale as a live system diagram. On load, the ring draws, pink nodes travel inward along their spokes, the soma takes on its gradient, violet nodes light, and the black chain fires outward, node by node, toward the headline column. Hovering or focusing any node names it. It takes about two seconds and happens once.

Everything else on the site is quiet: white space, black type, rigorous grid. The hero earns its boldness because the rest of the page doesn't compete.

## The recurring device: the axon

A single 2px vertical line runs down the left gutter of the homepage on desktop, from the hero's black chain to the final CTA. Each section is a node on it. Node colour encodes that section's pillar (pink for data sections, violet for AI, ink for business and product, the soma gradient for the one section where they meet). It's structural, not decorative: it tells you which part of the story you're in. On tablet and mobile it disappears; section headers carry a small node of the same colour instead.

## Brand personality

Intelligent, precise, confident, technical, human, reliable, ambitious. Never loud, hype-driven, or futuristic for its own sake. The voice of a principal engineer who has also run a P&L.

## Voice examples

Do:
> Your AI is only as good as the pipeline under it. We build the pipeline first.

Don't:
> Unlock the power of next-generation AI to supercharge your digital transformation journey.

## Logo usage

- Use the official vectors from the asset sheet. Variants: primary light, dark background, monochrome black, monochrome white knock-out (on plum), small-size icon (≤64px, simplified), horizontal lockup, stacked lockup, horizontal mono.
- Navbar: horizontal lockup, mark height 32px desktop, 28px mobile.
- Favicon and app icons: small-size icon variant only.
- Clear space: the width of one terminal node on all sides of the ring.
- Never recolour spokes outside the palette, rotate, stretch, add effects, or place the colour mark on a busy image.
